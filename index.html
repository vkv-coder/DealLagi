FAQ

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

❓Is DealLagi free?

Yes! Sign up is free. Plans are based on the number of items and platforms you track — pay only for what you need.

❓How will I get alerts?

When a major discount is detected on your item, you receive an instant alert directly on your mobile.

❓Can I choose which platforms to track?

Yes! When you add an item, you select the platform(s) you want monitored for that item.

❓How soon will my account be activated?

After signing up, our team reviews your registration within 24 hours. You'll be notified by email once your account is active.

❓Which cities are supported?

DealLagi works in all major Indian cities where Swiggy Instamart, Blinkit, and Zepto operate.
